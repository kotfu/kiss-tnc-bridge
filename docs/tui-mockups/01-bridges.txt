ktbctl tui: the Bridges view
============================

What you see first. One screen, three parts:

- the bridge tree: bridges, their ports, and the apps connected to them;
- a lower pane, showing the selected bridge's frames, or (Tab) details of
  the selected row;
- one line of keys for the selected row.

The selected row is in reverse video (shown here with ">").

RX and TX are activity lights. Each lights up for a moment when bytes move
(from the daemon's `activity` events), RX in green, TX in amber; "■" lit,
"□" dark. Without colour they still read as filled and empty.

Bridges: "●" running, "○" disabled. A bridge that can't be enabled yet says
what it's missing. A bridge from the static config file shows "static" and
its keys say what can't be done there.

Notes
-----

- WHERE says what the port talks to in the fewest words: the TNC and the
  adapter for a radio, the advertised name and adapter for BLE, the listen
  address for TCP.
- STATE for a TNC port is the link (connected, retrying in 8 s, not
  paired, waiting for wlan0, held up by 'ktbctl connect'); for an app port
  it's "N of M apps", or "advertising" / "listening" with none.
- The tree stays in config order. A stopped bridge folds to one line;
  → / ← open and fold any bridge.
- Frames in the lower pane are the selected bridge's; the Frames view (3)
  shows every bridge's, with more room.
