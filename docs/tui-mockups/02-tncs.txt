Notes
-----

- Each TNC lists every way it can be reached (Bluetooth, USB/serial), and
  for each whether it's usable now: paired or not, plugged in or not.
- Adapters show what they are (built in, USB product), as `setup` does, so
  two adapters on a Pi can be told apart. One named in config but not
  present is listed so you can see why a bridge is waiting.
- n scans (on the selected TNC's adapter, or ask which). The list fills in
  as devices are found, as in `setup`. ⏎ pairs the selected device; a sets
  it up as a new TNC by handing off to the setup wizard (see 05).
