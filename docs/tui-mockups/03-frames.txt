Notes
-----

- It follows the newest frame until you move up; then it holds still and
  the title says how many came in since. End (or G) goes back to
  following. Space pauses outright.
- / filters by text (a callsign, usually). b toggles between all bridges
  and the bridge selected in the Bridges view.
- rx is from a TNC (heard on the air); tx is to a TNC, and its line shows
  the app port it came from, as the Bridges view does. The decode pane
  names the app.
- The decode pane is what the daemon decodes for display; h toggles the
  raw KISS bytes in full.
- It keeps the last 1,000 frames.
