StickC Plus SE UI mockups

Round 1 for #28: three ways to lay out the 135×240 screen, in portrait and landscape, at the screen's real pixel size (zoomed). Text is Montserrat at 12, 14, 20 and 28 px, the sizes LVGL would use. The devices work: click B for the next page, click A once, twice, or hold it 3 s, and click Power to turn the screen off and on. The buttons above set what every device is showing.

To decide

  • Which layout: the state band, quiet, or the LED tile; or a mix (the quiet layout with a thin band, say).
  • Which page marker: 1/3, dots, an arrow pointing at B, or tabs. Each sits at the end of the top bar nearest B, so it points at the button that moves you on: the right end in portrait, the left end in landscape.
  • Portrait or landscape. Landscape gives two columns and a wider line (about 33 characters at 12 px, against 19), but the pages have to be shorter: 115 px under the top bar, against 220.
  • Three pages or two. Bridge and TNC overlap: both show the radio and its state. Two pages (Bridge with the radio's address, then System) would make B a toggle.
  • The Kenwood's number. The ATOM accepts it without asking. The mockup shows it, so you can check it against the radio, but still asks nothing: no yes or no, so A keeps the ATOM's gestures.
  • B while pairing or in authorization mode: the mockup ignores it (the screen belongs to the mode). It could change pages under the mode instead, or cancel it.
  • The red LED: the mockup flicks it for each frame to the radio (it will transmit) and blinks the ATOM's error pattern, since the end with the LED can be seen with the screen off or in a pouch.
  • Single press of A on an ordinary page: nothing but a wake, as the ATOM, or something of its own (scroll the apps list, say).
